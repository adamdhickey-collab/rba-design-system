Using an image

RuleRequirement
CroppingCrop freely, but keep the subject. Don't crop so tight that a photograph of people becomes a photograph of a shoulder.
ColorUse them as supplied. No filters, no duotone, no recoloring to force a photo toward the palette.
Type over imageOnly over a quiet, dark area, in the reversed logo's white. If it needs a heavy scrim to read, pick a different image.
ResolutionCheck the dimensions on the tile. Don't scale a small image up — request a larger one instead.
RightsEverything here is cleared for RBA use. Images from anywhere else are not, including ones that look similar.
