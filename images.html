Brand images

A reviewed shortlist of fifty Adobe Stock candidates, ranked within five categories. Nothing here is licensed yet — this is the board for deciding what to buy.

shopping_cart

These are candidates, not assets. None of them is licensed. Every picture below is a watermarked Adobe Stock comp, saved for review — that is what the watermark across each one means, and it is the reason none of these files may be used in anything that ships. The shortlist carries the reasoning, the contributor and the dimensions, and every card links through to the asset on Adobe Stock. Each row also needs its AI disclosure confirmed before licensing; the review found none stated, which is not the same as none existing.

Once an image is licensed, replace the comp in assets/images/shortlist/ with the clean file, keeping the Adobe ID as the name — 1904000970.jpg. The card picks it up on the next load: no manifest edit and no rebuild, and the watermark disappears on its own.

Before licensing

CheckWhat to confirm
AI disclosureThe review found no explicit disclosure on any asset page, which is not the same as confirming there is none. Check each one on Adobe Stock before buying — the selection brief excludes AI-generated imagery.
Licence typeSeveral picks are Stocksy or other premium-collection assets and are not covered by a standard subscription. Confirm the tier before assuming a credit covers it.
Model releasesEvery shortlisted image contains recognisable people. Confirm the release covers the intended use, including any paid or out-of-home placement.
ResolutionDimensions are on each card. Check the crop you actually intend still leaves enough pixels; don’t buy on the raw number alone.
DuplicationStandard-collection images are licensed to anyone. For anything carrying the brand — homepage, campaign hero — prefer the premium picks, which are far less widely used.

Using an image

RuleRequirement
CroppingCrop freely, but keep the subject. Don’t crop so tight that a photograph of people becomes a photograph of a shoulder. Each card carries the reviewer’s crop note.
ColorUse them as supplied. No filters, no duotone, no recoloring to force a photo toward the palette.
Type over imageOnly over a quiet, dark area, in the reversed logo’s white. If it needs a heavy scrim to read, pick a different image.
ResolutionDon’t scale a small image up — go back to the source and take a larger render.
RightsOnly licensed images may ship. A shortlist card is not a licence, and neither is a watermarked comp.
