01 · Foundations

Colors

Seven brand colors and five neutrals, from the RBA Design & Brand Guidelines v1.0. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Primary

Midnight is the interface anchor and carries most of the surface area. RBA Red is the mark's color and works as an accent — a headline rule, a highlight, a single emphasised number — never as a large fill or a button background.

Neutrals

Text, rules and recessed surfaces. Black is #333333, not #000000 — pure black is not in the palette.

Gradient

The two-stop wash used behind heroes and header bands. Always this pair, always light-to-mid — it sits under type, so it can never get dark enough to fight the text on top of it.

#E6F8FB → #9DDBF6, 135°

02 · Foundations

Typography

Two typefaces. Montserrat carries everything functional; Libre Caslon Text is reserved for editorial display. Both are Google Fonts, so there is nothing to license and nothing to install.

Montserrat

Sans · 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 / 800

Headings, body copy, interface, labels. Everything unless there's a reason otherwise.

Libre Caslon Text

Serif · 400 / 400 italic / 700

Editorial display only — a pull quote, a report cover, a single statement headline. Never body copy, never UI.

Rules

RuleRequirement
Clear spaceThe height of the monogram on all four sides. Nothing enters it — not type, not an image edge, not another logo.
Minimum size24px monogram height on screen, 0.25in in print. Below that the wordmark stops resolving.
Which colorwayPrimary wherever it fits. Reversed on midnight, on black, and over photography. One-color black or red only where the process genuinely allows a single ink.
Over photographyReversed, on a part of the image that is dark and quiet. If the mark needs a scrim to be legible, the photo is wrong for it.
NeverRedraw or retype it. Recolor outside these four. Stretch, skew, rotate, outline, or add a shadow or glow. Rebuild it from a screenshot. Place it on a background that breaks its contrast.
